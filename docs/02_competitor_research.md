# Day 2 — Competitor Research & Market Analysis

Researched 4 real products that solve a similar problem: verifying product authenticity via smartphone scan, with a focus on agriculture-relevant examples.

---

## 1. Bayer Seal Scan App / FarmRise (Bayer Crop Science, India)

**What it does:** Lets Indian farmers scan the QR code on a Bayer "Safety Seal" to confirm authenticity of Bayer Crop Science products, providing instant feedback on originality.

**Does well:**
- Directly targets the exact use case Farmer Dost is solving — farmer-facing, agri-input authentication.
- Simple, single-purpose flow: scan → instant result.
- Free, simple, quick, and reliable — low friction for farmers.

**Does poorly:**
- Single-brand only — verifies Bayer products exclusively, useless for other fertilizer/pesticide brands.
- No cross-brand database, no dealer trust ratings, no community reporting feature.
- No advisory or crop-support features beyond authentication.

**Takeaway for Farmer Dost:** Validates that farmers already have exposure to "scan to verify" apps — but the market gap is a **multi-brand, independent** verifier.

---

## 2. Checko (IIT Kanpur-incubated startup)

**What it does:** Uses a 3D signature/pattern authentication technology; farmers scan with Checko app. Adopted by Chambal Fertilisers to secure crop protection products.

**Does well:**
- Purpose-built for Indian agri-input counterfeit problem, with real fertilizer-company adoption (Chambal).
- Technology is harder to clone than standard printed QR code.
- Strong storytelling around farmer trust.

**Does poorly:**
- Manufacturer-driven, opt-in system — only works for brands that adopt Checko labels.
- No visible farmer-facing features beyond verification (no advisory, no dealer reporting).
- Slow adoption leaving regional brands uncovered.

**Takeaway for Farmer Dost:** Confirms real market demand — but shows weakness of manufacturer-only rollouts. An independent farmer-first app with a broader database covers this gap.

---

## 3. Scantrust

**What it does:** Global general-purpose anti-counterfeit app. Scan QR code to check authenticity, origin, and report questionable products to brand in real time.

**Does well:**
- Includes a **report-to-brand** feature.
- Shows rich product info beyond genuine/fake (origin, production date).

**Does poorly:**
- Generic, not farmer-specific — no agricultural context or offline rural support.
- Reporting goes to brand, not to a farmer-protecting authority or community.

**Takeaway for Farmer Dost:** Borrow reporting pattern, but route reports toward farmer protection and dealer accountability.

---

## 4. Chekkit App

**What it does:** Identify fake products via QR/barcode scanning, report counterfeit products, and earn rewards (airtime/cash).

**Does well:**
- Gamifies verification with rewards.
- Web-based no-download alternative option.

**Does poorly:**
- Works only on scratch-off sticker labels carrying Chekkit's brand.
- Reward model risks spam reports if unmoderated.

**Takeaway for Farmer Dost:** Consider lightweight web fallback for basic feature access. Keep reporting focused on farmer safety.

---

## Strategic Gaps & Farmer Dost Positioning

| Gap in existing solutions | How Farmer Dost Differentiates |
|---|---|
| Single-brand / manufacturer-locked | Independent, multi-brand verification database |
| Reports protect brand reputation | Reports route to dealer accountability & farmer community warnings |
| Lacks localized agricultural context | Designed specifically for Punjab/Indian farmers (voice, multi-language, clear visual cues) |
