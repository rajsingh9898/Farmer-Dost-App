# Day 3 — User Research & Ground Interviews

## Overview

Target Goal: Interview 3–5 small-scale farmers and agricultural dealers in rural Punjab regarding counterfeit fertilizer/pesticide experiences and smartphone usage.

**Current Progress**: 2 interviews completed (Farmer & Local Dealer). 2 additional interviews scheduled.

---

## Interview 1: Gurdev Singh (Wheat & Paddy Farmer, Ludhiana District)

- **Land Holding**: 6 acres
- **Smartphone Literacy**: Medium (uses WhatsApp, YouTube, and Paytm regularly)
- **Key Experience with Counterfeit Inputs**:
  - Purchased a 50kg bag of alleged Zinc/Urea mix last season from a local sub-dealer.
  - Noticed no crop growth improvement after application; later learned from neighboring farmers that the dealer was selling diluted batch bags.
- **Feedback on App Idea**:
  - *"If I can just point my phone camera at the bag code before paying the dealer, it will save me thousands of rupees."*
  - Emphasized that results **must be instant** and show big green/red indicators because reading fine English text is difficult.

---

## Interview 2: Ramesh Kumar (Authorized Fertilizer & Agri-Input Dealer, Khanna)

- **Business**: Licensed retail shop for KRIBHCO, IFFCO, and regional pesticide brands.
- **Key Insight**:
  - Legitimate dealers are hurt by rogue unlicensed vendors selling fake pesticides at undercut prices.
  - Confirmed that major companies print QR codes/barcodes on batch packaging, but farmers rarely check or know how to verify them.
- **Feedback on App Idea**:
  - Supports an independent verification app because it builds customer trust for honest dealers.
  - Warned that reporting must collect product code and shop location accurately to prevent malicious false reports against honest shops.

---

## Key Synthesis & Design Requirements

1. **Visual-First Verification Result**: Large green checkmark or red warning icon — cannot rely solely on text.
2. **Instant Verification**: Latency must be under 2 seconds over 3G/4G networks.
3. **Structured Reporting**: Include optional dealer location & photo/note to make fake reports actionable for authorities.
4. **Scan History**: Farmers want to re-check what products they bought during the previous cropping season.
